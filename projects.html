Magnetic Alignment Dynamics for Drone Payload Docking Report

Modeled a two-axis magnetic docking connector for a heavy-lift payload drone using nonlinear rotational differential equation dynamics. Developed a two-axis ball-joint alignment model for pitch and yaw motion, where magnetic restoring torque drives the connector toward alignment and damping reduces oscillatory motion during docking. Delivered a full technical report with derivations.

MATLAB · ODE Modeling · Rotational Dynamics · Drone Systems

Avionics Box Structural & Thermal Analysis

Analyzed an aircraft avionics box support structure across multiple flight conditions (takeoff, cruise up to Mach 2.2, turbulence, landing). Performed limit/ultimate load calculations, normal & shear stress analysis, principal stresses, and max shear stress. Sized supply/exhaust air paths by estimating heat transfer, defining boundary conditions, and modeling temperature distribution to ensure safe operating ranges. Delivered 3D CAD models, 2D drawings, and a full technical report with recommendations for material selection, weight reduction, and manufacturability improvements.

Causal Analysis of Aviation Accident Lethality

Analyzed 5,268 fatal aviation accidents from 1908–2009 to identify contributing factors associated with high-lethality outcomes. Converted unstructured crash summaries into structured cause categories using keyword classification, then applied causal discovery techniques to move beyond correlation-based safety analysis. Used FCI causal discovery with chi-square conditional independence testing, domain-informed constraints, and regression-based causal effect estimation to evaluate how factors such as CFIT, weather, military operation status, fuel issues, and mechanical failure influenced accident severity.

6-Person Team · Python · Causal Inference · Regression Analysis
